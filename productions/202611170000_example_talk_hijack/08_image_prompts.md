# 08 Image Prompts — example_talk_hijack (10 beats, B01~B10)

창작 예시 「내 얘기만 하면 자기 얘기?」 — 남편의 대화 가로채기 화법. Korean webtoon story style, character-consistency via reference lock.

## COMMON_BASE
Modern Korean apartment interior throughout (living room, kitchen, near a window). Warm domestic daylight or soft evening lamp light, clean vertical webtoon composition, emotionally readable faces, consistent fictional characters across every beat. Contemporary Korea. Faces allowed (fictional persons, kept consistent). No physical contact, no violence — the conflict is entirely verbal (a habit of turning every conversation back to himself). Flat blank walls or simple domestic backdrops instead of any signage.

## CHARACTER_GUIDE
- **NARRATOR** (화자, 아내): woman in her early 30s, long straight black hair tied in a low ponytail, dusty-rose pink knit sweater, gentle expressive face. Expression arcs from tired/hopeful to quietly hurt to puzzled to calmly resolute. The protagonist who slowly realizes a pattern.
- **HUSBAND** (남편): man in his early-to-mid 30s, short wavy brown hair, no glasses, navy blue hoodie or plain white shirt, average build. Expression arcs from eager and talkative to oblivious to defensive to visibly frustrated (when the tables turn). Not a cartoon villain — just habitually self-focused, friendly-looking even while interrupting.

## NEGATIVE
no text, no letters, no numbers, no signage, no brand names, no logos, no readable screens, no readable documents, no hand close-ups, no ring close-ups, no finger detail, no watermark, no captions, no physical contact, no raised hands, no violence, no speech bubbles, no empty dialogue balloons, no thought bubbles, no framed wall posters with text, no wall art with lettering.
